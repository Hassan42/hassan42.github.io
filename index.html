Hassan Atwi

PhD student in Informatics at USI Università della Svizzera italiana, Lugano.

I am a PhD student in Informatics and software engineer in the DESIGN research group at the Software Institute, USI, supervised by Prof. Cesare Pautasso. I specialise in blockchain-based business process automation, from model-driven tooling to smart-contract deployment.

I have developed smart contract engineering methods for business processes and message ordering mechanisms for distributed systems, with results in eight peer-reviewed publications. This includes BPMN4BC, a model-driven extension of the BPMN standard for blockchain-aware process modelling, with a compiler that turns process models into deployable Solidity smart contracts.

I also enjoy turning research ideas into working software and, as a teaching assistant, guiding students through full-stack web projects.

Projects

Map Monitor (2026)
TypeScript, React, MapLibre, Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Firebase
Built and deployed a full-stack web platform for mapping conflict events and territories on an interactive timeline, with versioned drafts and publishing, and shareable public maps.

BPMN4BC (2025)
JavaScript, Solidity, Hardhat, bpmn.io, Node.js, Express.js
Developed a compiler service that parses BPMN4BC diagrams and automatically generates, compiles and deploys the matching Solidity smart contracts.

Process-Aware Consensus (2024)
JavaScript, Solidity, Hardhat, Docker, Bash
Automated a distributed multi-node test environment to benchmark transaction-ordering strategies for latency, cost and censorship resistance.

PyRef (2021)
Python, pandas
Built a static analysis tool that detects 11 types of code refactoring from the Git history of Python projects.
